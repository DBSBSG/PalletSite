BUYING GUIDE

Before you bid
or buy.

Check the manifest

Look for unit counts, brands, and condition grades. A returns pallet may include damaged or incomplete items.

Calculate the delivered cost

Factor in buyer fees, tax, freight, and liftgate or residential delivery charges.

Read the supplier’s terms

Check resale requirements, pickup deadlines, and what happens if the delivered lot differs from its manifest.
